## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Syrmos gives you instant departure countdowns for Greek public transport. Athens metro, tram and suburban railway, the airport buses, and now Thessaloniki, all in one simple app.

Whether you are commuting every day, changing lines, or racing for the next train or airport bus, Syrmos helps you check departures, plan a journey, browse timetables, view station details and understand your route faster.

WHAT YOU GET

• Live departure countdowns for Athens rail stations
• Direction-based departures, so you know which way your train is going
• GPS detection to find your nearest station automatically
• Metro Lines 1, 2, 3, Tram T6/T7 and Suburban Railway services
• Live airport buses for Athens (X93, X95, X96, X97) and Thessaloniki, on the map and at the stop
• Interactive transit map with supported live train and bus positions
• Journey planning with step-by-step guidance and leave-by reminders
• Full timetable viewer per station
• Station details with connecting lines and interchange information
• Fare information for metro, tram and suburban railway journeys
• Quick access to Hellenic Train ticket pages for Suburban Railway services
• Ichnos community status, so riders can flag a crowded or disrupted stop
• Multilingual interface in English, Greek, Albanian and Italian

WIDGETS AND APPLE WATCH

Your next train, without opening the app.

• Home Screen widgets: Next Train, Live Departures, Near Me, All Lines Status, and a Weather + Alerts view
• Lock Screen Live Activity and Dynamic Island countdown for the train you are tracking
• Apple Watch app with the next departures for your station, plus corner, circular and rectangular complications
• Every widget projects offline from the schedule that ships with the app, and follows your language

ARIADNE, YOUR OFFLINE ASSISTANT

Ask Syrmos about Greek trains in plain language, fully offline, in English, Greek, Albanian or Italian. "When is the last train home from Syntagma?" "How do I get to the airport?" Ariadne answers from the schedule on your device. She never invents train times.

WORKS OFFLINE

All schedule data ships with the app. Underground with no signal? Syrmos still helps you check departures, stations, timetables and your planned journey.

LIVE TRACKING

See supported train and airport-bus services moving on the map and follow your journey across the network.

WEATHER

Current conditions on your Home screen, so you know it is raining before the open-air tram stop tells you in person.

FARES AND TICKETS

Check fare information directly in the app. For Suburban Railway tickets, Syrmos can redirect you to the official Hellenic Train website for purchase. Syrmos is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Hellenic Train, OASA, STASY, OSETH or the airport bus operators.

BUILT FOR GREECE

Fast, simple and focused on one thing: helping you catch your next train or bus.

## What's New (v3.0.0)
Three-point-oh. The one that leaves the platform and actually goes somewhere.

Syrmos is no longer just "when is my train." It now plans the whole trip, catches the airport bus, and crosses the border to Thessaloniki.

• Airport buses, live. X93, X95, X96 and X97 in Athens, plus Thessaloniki, on the map and at the stop, with real positions when the operator is sharing them. Tap a stop to see what is actually coming, not a poster from 2015.

• Thessaloniki. Because Athens does not get to have all the transit anxiety.

• Journeys. Plan a route, get step-by-step guidance, and set a leave-by reminder so your phone nudges you off the couch at exactly the right minute. A Live Activity rides along on the Lock Screen.

• Ichnos, community status. See and flag a crowded or disrupted stop, anonymously, so the next rider is not surprised the way you just were.

• Italian joins English, Greek and Albanian. Quattro lingue. Ariadne answers offline in all of them and still refuses to invent train times, which keeps her more trustworthy than most directions given in Monastiraki.

Widgets, the Apple Watch app, complications and the Dynamic Island countdown are all still here, still offline, still in your language.

Thank you for keeping Syrmos near the top of Navigation in Greece. Still calmer than finding a seat on Line 1 at 08:30.
