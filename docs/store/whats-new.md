# What's New — release notes

Trilingual store release notes. Albanian (SQ) is first-class. Keep each within the
App Store (4000 char) / Play (500 char) limits; the short forms below are Play-safe.

## This release: all of Greece by rail

Syrmos grew from an Athens app into a rail companion for the whole country:
Thessaloniki metro + suburban, the InterCity line between Athens and Thessaloniki,
and Patras suburban. Everything still works offline, underground, with no signal.

---

### Albanian / Shqip (primary)

**E shkurtër (Play):**
Syrmos tani mbulon të gjithë Greqinë me tren. Metroja e Selanikut, hekurudha
lokale (Larisa, Florina, Sindos, Serres–Drama), InterCity Athinë–Selanik dhe
hekurudha lokale e Patrës. Gjithçka funksionon offline, edhe në tunel. Ariadna, ju
përgjigjet për nisjet dhe itineraret në shqip, greqisht ose anglisht.

**E gjatë (App Store):**
Syrmos nuk është më vetëm për Athinën. Ky përditësim sjell të gjithë rrjetin
hekurudhor të Greqisë:

• Metroja e Selanikut — Linja 1 është aktive; Linja 2 (Kalamaria) shfaqet e
  zbehtë derisa të hapet.
• Hekurudha lokale e Selanikut — Larisa, Edesa/Florina, Sindos dhe Serres–Drama,
  me orare të sakta për çdo stacion.
• InterCity Athinë–Selanik dhe Athinë–Lianokladi.
• Hekurudha lokale e Patrës — degët Kaminia dhe Rio, plus autobusi lidhës i
  Kato Achaia-s.

Të gjitha oraret janë marrë nga burimet zyrtare të Hellenic Train dhe ruhen si kohë
të sakta për çdo stacion — asgjë e vlerësuar. Një linjë e ndërtuar por ende e
pahapur shfaqet e zbehtë dhe nuk tregon nisje derisa të fillojë shërbimi.

Ashtu si gjithmonë: funksionon offline, në tunel, pa sinjal. Shqipja mbetet
gjuhë e plotë krahas greqishtes dhe anglishtes.

### English

**Short (Play):**
Syrmos now covers all of Greece by rail. Thessaloniki metro, suburban lines
(Larisa, Florina, Sindos, Serres–Drama), Athens–Thessaloniki InterCity, and Patras
suburban. Works offline, underground. Ariadne answers departures and routes in
English, Greek or Albanian.

**Long (App Store):**
Syrmos is no longer just for Athens. This update brings Greece's whole rail
network:

• Thessaloniki Metro — Line 1 is live; Line 2 (Kalamaria) shows greyed until it
  opens.
• Thessaloniki suburban — Larisa, Edessa/Florina, Sindos and Serres–Drama, with
  exact per-station times.
• InterCity Athens–Thessaloniki and Athens–Leianokladi.
• Patras suburban — the Kaminia and Rio branches, plus the Kato Achaia connecting
  bus.

Every timetable is taken from official Hellenic Train sources and stored as exact
per-station times — nothing estimated. A line that is built but not yet open shows
greyed and lists no departures until service begins.

As always: works offline, underground, with no signal. Albanian stays a
first-class language alongside Greek and English.

**Εκτενής (App Store):**
Το Syrmos δεν είναι πια μόνο για την Αθήνα. Αυτή η ενημέρωση φέρνει όλο το
σιδηροδρομικό δίκτυο της Ελλάδας:

• Μετρό Θεσσαλονίκης — η Γραμμή 1 είναι ενεργή· η Γραμμή 2 (Καλαμαριά) εμφανίζεται
  αχνή μέχρι να ανοίξει.
• Προαστιακός Θεσσαλονίκης — Λάρισα, Έδεσσα/Φλώρινα, Σίνδος και Σέρρες–Δράμα, με
  ακριβείς ώρες ανά σταθμό.
• InterCity Αθήνα–Θεσσαλονίκη και Αθήνα–Λιανοκλάδι.
• Προαστιακός Πάτρας — οι κλάδοι Καμίνια και Ρίο, και το συνδετικό λεωφορείο της

Όλα τα δρομολόγια προέρχονται από επίσημες πηγές της Hellenic Train και

κατασκευή εμφανίζεται αχνή και δεν δείχνει δρομολόγια μέχρι να ξεκινήσει.

Όπως πάντα: λειτουργεί εκτός σύνδεσης, στο τούνελ, χωρίς σήμα. Τα αλβανικά
παραμένουν πλήρης γλώσσα δίπλα στα ελληνικά και τα αγγλικά.
