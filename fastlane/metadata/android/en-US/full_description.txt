Syrmos tells you when your next train or bus actually leaves. Athens metro, tram and suburban rail, the airport buses, and now Thessaloniki.

- Live countdowns and your nearest station by GPS
- Airport buses (X93, X95, X96, X97) and Thessaloniki, on the map and at the stop
- Plan a route, with guidance and a leave-now reminder
- Fares, timetables and station details
- Ichnos: see and flag a crowded or disrupted stop
- Widgets, Apple Watch and a Lock Screen countdown
- English, Greek, Albanian and Italian

Everything works offline from the schedule in the app. Ask Ariadne in plain words and she answers from that schedule, never a made-up time.

Not affiliated with Hellenic Train, OASA, STASY or OSETH.
