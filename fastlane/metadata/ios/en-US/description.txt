Syrmos tells you when your next train, tram or bus actually leaves, all over Greece. It works offline from the schedule inside the app and never shows a made-up time.

WHERE IT WORKS
- Athens: Metro Lines 1, 2 and 3 including the airport branch, Tram T6 and T7, Suburban Railway A1 to A4
- Athens airport buses X93, X95, X96 and X97, with live positions and arrival times when the operator shares them
- Thessaloniki: Metro Lines 1 and 2, the X3 and 2X airport buses, and suburban trains to Larisa, Florina, Sindos and Serres-Drama
- Patras: suburban trains Patras-Kaminia and Patras-Rio, the Kato Achaia link and the university buses
- National rail: Intercity Athens-Thessaloniki, Athens-Leianokladi, Alexandroupoli-Ormenio and Katakolo-Pyrgos-Olympia, plus the scenic Pelion and Diakopto-Kalavryta railways when they run
- Hellenic Train connecting buses: Kiato-Patras, Volos-Larisa, Paleofarsalos-Kalambaka, Tithorea-Lamia-Stylida and Drama-Xanthi-Alexandroupoli

NEW IN 3.0.2
- Station closures: closed stations and trains passing without stopping show on the map, the station screen and in your routes, with the hours and any replacement bus. The planner routes around them.
- Next train near you: an optional notification as you reach a station, with the next trains each way and an honest heads-up ("the next one is in 2 minutes, but another comes in 10, no need to run").
- Leave on time: calendar events with a place get a reminder telling you when to leave. Flights default to two hours at the airport.
- Announcements from Syrmos: important service news, such as a station closed by police order, as a notification in your language.

EVERY DAY
- Live countdowns and your nearest station by GPS
- Plan a route with step-by-step guidance, leave-by reminders and a live journey on the Lock Screen
- Fares, timetables, accessibility and station details
- Live service alerts from the operators
- Ichnos: see and anonymously flag a crowded or disrupted stop
- Ask Ariadne in plain words; she answers from the real schedule
- Widgets, Apple Watch and a Lock Screen countdown
- English, Greek, Albanian and Italian

PRIVACY
No account. Your location and calendar stay on your phone. Announcements use an anonymous notification token and nothing else.
