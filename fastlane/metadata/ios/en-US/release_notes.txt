Three-point-oh. The one that leaves the platform and actually goes somewhere.

Syrmos is no longer just "when is my train." It now plans the whole trip, catches the airport bus, and crosses to Thessaloniki.

• Airport buses, live. X93, X95, X96 and X97 in Athens, plus Thessaloniki, on the map and at the stop, with real positions when the operator is sharing them. Tap a stop to see what is actually coming.

• Thessaloniki. Because Athens does not get to have all the transit anxiety.

• Journeys. Plan a route, get step-by-step guidance, and set a leave-by reminder so your phone nudges you off the couch at the right minute. A Live Activity rides along on the Lock Screen.

• Ichnos, community status. See and flag a crowded or disrupted stop, anonymously, so the next rider is not surprised the way you just were.

• Italian joins English, Greek and Albanian. Ariadne answers offline in all four and still refuses to invent train times.

Widgets, the Apple Watch app, complications and the Dynamic Island countdown are all still here, offline, in your language.

Thank you for keeping Syrmos near the top of Navigation in Greece.
