Syrmos

The next train, first

Open Syrmos and the answer is already there: the next departure from the station near you, how many minutes away, and the two after it.

Plan a trip from A to B, with every change and stop. Syrmos walks you through it and reminds you when to leave.

Metro, tram, suburban rail and airport buses on one map, each in its own line colour, with trains moving along the track.

iPhone, with widgets and Apple Watch Android phones

What it does

Departures by station

Every line through a station, grouped by direction, in minutes and clock times. Interchanges and step-free access show at the top.

  • Live
  • Scheduled
  • Estimated
  • Offline snapshot

Every time says where it came from

Live is a real-time feed. Scheduled is the published timetable. Estimated is projected from it. Offline snapshot is stored on your device.

Journeys, step by step

Routes with their changes, how tight each one is, guidance on the way, and a reminder when it is time to leave.

Still there underground

Timetables and stations are stored with the app, so departures appear when the signal drops. Live updates need a connection.

Fares you can check

Full and reduced prices from official operator sources. Syrmos sells no tickets; purchase links open the operator's site.

Ask Ariadne

Ask in plain words: the last train tonight, a trip from A to B, a ticket price. She answers from Syrmos timetables and fares, and never invents a time.

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes, for stored information. The iOS and Android apps carry the timetables and stations. The web app stores them after one online visit, as long as your browser keeps site data. Live positions and alerts need a connection.

Live or scheduled?

Live times come from operators' real-time feeds where they exist. Scheduled times are the published timetable. Estimated positions are projected from it. Every time carries its label.

Can I buy tickets in Syrmos?

No. Syrmos shows fares and links to the operator, such as Hellenic Train, where you buy the ticket.

Which languages?

English, Greek, Albanian and Italian, in the app and in Ariadne.

What does it know about me?

Nothing it sends anywhere. No account, no tracking. Location is optional and stays on your device to find nearby stations. Read the privacy policy.
