How it works

Built for the moment you need it.

Three everyday questions, answered without digging through timetables.

  1. 01

    See what’s next.

    Search a station and see departures grouped by line and direction, with the next few times together. Interchanges and accessibility show at the top.

    • Live comes from a real-time feed.
    • Scheduled is the published timetable.
    • Estimated is a position projected from the timetable.
    • Offline snapshot is stored data shown without a connection.

  2. 02

    See the bigger picture.

    Metro, tram, suburban rail and airport buses share one map, each in its own line colour. Tap a station for its lines and connections.

    Vehicle markers use real-time positions where a feed exists. Other lines are estimated from the timetable and labelled that way.

  3. 03

    Keep the essentials with you.

    Schedules and station information are stored with the app, so departures still appear when the signal drops underground.

    Live updates need a connection. On the web, offline use starts after one online visit and depends on your browser keeping the stored data.

Lines follow their track geometry, stations sit on their lines, and vehicles move along the network. Suspended and under-construction lines are drawn greyed out.

Fares

Ticket prices for Athens, Thessaloniki, Patras suburban and suburban rail, with full and reduced prices, taken from official operator sources. Intercity fares are shown as priced at booking.

Syrmos does not sell tickets. Purchase links open the operator’s own website.

  • iOS
  • Android
  • Web

Ask in plain language

Ariadne answers transit questions such as next departures, the last train tonight, a trip from A to B, service alerts and ticket prices. Answers come from Syrmos schedule and fare data, not from guesswork.

Ariadne is a transit assistant, not a general chatbot. It can misunderstand a question, so check important journeys.

Coverage

From the Athens metro to the national network.

Syrmos covers these networks today. It doesn’t cover every transport service in Greece.

Athens

  • M1M2M3 Metro Lines 1, 2 and 3
  • T6T7 Tram
  • A1A4 Suburban Railway, lines A1 to A4
  • Airport metro and suburban connections

Thessaloniki

  • Metro Lines 1 and 2
  • Suburban rail to Larisa, Florina, Sindos and Serres to Drama
  • Airport buses X3 and 2X connecting to the metro

Patras

  • Suburban rail to Kaminia and to Rio
  • Connecting buses, including the University loop

National rail

  • Intercity Athens to Thessaloniki and regional services
  • Rail-replacement buses such as Kiato to Patra and Volos to Larisa
  • The Pelion Railway, which runs seasonally

Services that are suspended or under construction are marked that way on the map, never as running.

Questions

Does Syrmos work offline?

Yes, for stored information. Schedules and station details are stored with the iOS and Android apps. The web app for laptops and desktop computers stores them after your first online visit, as long as your browser keeps site data. Live updates, real-time positions and service alerts need a connection. Offline times are labelled so you can tell them apart.

Which regions are supported?

Athens metro, tram and suburban railway; Thessaloniki metro, suburban rail and airport buses; Patras suburban rail and buses; and national rail corridors, including rail-replacement buses. Syrmos doesn’t cover every transport service in Greece.

What is the difference between live and scheduled times?

Live times and positions come from real-time feeds where operators provide them. Scheduled times come from the published timetable. Estimated positions are projected from the timetable when no live feed is available. Every time in Syrmos carries its label.

Can I buy tickets in Syrmos?

No. Syrmos shows fare information and links to the relevant operator, such as Hellenic Train, where you complete the purchase.
