Answers

Common questions.

Does Syrmos work offline?

Yes, for stored information. Schedules and station details ship with the iOS and Android apps; the web app stores them after your first online visit. Live times, positions and alerts need a connection, and every time is labelled live or scheduled.

Which regions are supported?

Athens metro, tram and suburban railway and the airport buses; Thessaloniki, including its airport buses; and national rail corridors. Syrmos does not cover every transport service in Greece.

Can I buy tickets in Syrmos?

No. Syrmos shows fare information and links to the operator, such as Hellenic Train, where you complete the purchase.

Do I need an account, and what happens to my data?

No account. Location is optional and used only on your device to find nearby stations. Community (Ichnos) reports are anonymous. To remove everything, delete the app.

What is Ariadne?

The in-app assistant. She answers in plain language, fully offline, from the schedule on your device, in English, Greek, Albanian or Italian, and never invents a time.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in the app and choose English, Greek, Albanian or Italian. Widgets and the Apple Watch app follow the same choice.
